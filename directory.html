Decide who gets the unit with a consistent, documented process — apps, ID, income, background/credit, and fair criteria you can explain.

  • Placeholder: shared criteria sheet so every applicant is judged the same way

Forms

  • Consent for background/credit check
  • Adverse action / denial notice template (when you use consumer reports)

Vendors

  • Placeholder: screening service; optional local credit/reference help

Checklist

  • Collect app fee only where allowed and disclose it
  • Verify income and ID; run checks with written consent

Local notes — Tahlequah / Wagoner / Tulsa Watch city/county fair housing and any local licensing that affects how you advertise. Tulsa market runs faster than Tahlequah — same criteria, different timeline pressure. Lake STR guests are not the same as residential tenants; don’t mix playbooks.

Late rent

Stay calm, stay documented: when rent is late, what notice to send, how you communicate, and when you escalate — without making it personal.

Recommended tools

  • Placeholder: ledger in your PM software or a simple spreadsheet you never skip
  • Placeholder: dated notice templates (email + paper as needed)

Forms

  • Written demand for past-due rent under 41 O.S. §131 — tenant generally has 5 days after written demand to pay before you can terminate (confirm current statute)
  • What the statute says, with citations: Oklahoma 5-day notice to pay or quit (RentStatute)
  • Payment plan agreement (only if you choose to offer one)

Vendors

  • Placeholder: landlord-tenant attorney for chronic nonpayment; process server when required

Checklist

  • Confirm ledger (credits, partial payments, fees)
  • Send notice on your published timeline; keep proof of delivery
  • Do not accept “side deals” that contradict the lease unless you amend in writing
  • Know the next legal step before you threaten it
Local notes — Tahlequah / Wagoner / Tulsa County court practices differ — learn your courthouse path before you need it. Partial payments can complicate notice periods; get counsel if unsure. STR cancellations/no-shows are platform policy issues, not residential late-rent notices.
Move-out & security deposits

Walkthrough, photos, deductions that you can defend, and deposit return on the Oklahoma timeline. Most fights start with bad move-in docs.

Recommended tools

  • Placeholder: photo/video folder per unit (move-in vs move-out)
  • Placeholder: deposit accounting worksheet

Forms

  • Move-out notice acknowledgment / forwarding address request
  • Itemized written statement of deductions — delivered by return-receipt mail or in person (41 O.S. §115)
  • What the statute says, with citations: Oklahoma security deposit rules (RentStatute)
  • Final walkthrough checklist

Vendors

  • Placeholder: turnover cleaner, carpet cleaner, painter, trash-out crew

Checklist

  • Compare to move-in condition — normal wear vs damage
  • Hold deposits in an Oklahoma escrow account at a federally insured institution (§115)
  • Itemize deductions; return the balance within 45 days after the tenancy ends, possession is returned, and the tenant makes a written demand (§115 — confirm current statute)
  • Rekey after possession returned
  • Schedule make-ready immediately (empty weeks cost money)
Local notes — Tahlequah / Wagoner / Tulsa Deposit rules come from 41 O.S. §115 — the 45-day clock doesn’t start until the tenant’s written demand. Informational only; confirm current statute or ask counsel. Rural properties: document wells, septic, outbuildings. Lake STRs: security deposits/holds often run through Airbnb/VRBO; follow platform + your house rules.

STR vs long-term

Fort Gibson Lake Airbnbs and residential leases share “property ops” but differ on agreements, taxes, cleaning, insurance, and neighbor/HOA rules. Don’t copy-paste one playbook onto the other.

Recommended tools

  • LTR: lease + PM/rent tools
  • STR: Airbnb/VRBO (or similar), dynamic pricing optional, channel calendar sync
  • Both: shared maintenance vendor list

Forms

  • LTR: residential lease + addenda
  • STR: house rules, occupancy limits, quiet hours, boat/dock rules if any

Vendors

  • STR: turnover cleaners who can hit same-day windows; linen/restock supplier
  • LTR: make-ready crew between tenancies

Checklist

  • Confirm local STR rules / lodging tax / registration before listing
  • Separate insurance conversation for STR vs dwelling fire / landlord policy
  • Never treat a weekend guest like a month-to-month tenant (or vice versa)
Local notes — Tahlequah / Wagoner / Tulsa Fort Gibson Lake (Wagoner area): seasonal demand, weather, and dock/lake access drive reviews and wear. Lake rental ops need tight turnover SOPs. Tulsa LTR: different applicant pool and pricing. Always re-check city/county STR ordinances — they move.
Eviction basics (Oklahoma — informational)

Know the path before you’re angry: notice → filing → court → writ. Self-help (changing locks, shutting off utilities to force someone out) is how landlords lose.

Recommended tools

  • Placeholder: attorney who does landlord-tenant in your county
  • Placeholder: calendar of notice and court dates

Forms

  • Proper statutory notice for the issue (nonpayment vs other)
  • Statute overview with citations: Oklahoma Landlord-Tenant Act guide (RentStatute)
  • Court filing packet (county-specific)

Vendors

  • Landlord-tenant attorney; process server; locksmith after lawful possession

Checklist

  • Do not lock out or remove belongings without a court order
  • Serve notices correctly; keep copies
  • Bring ledger and lease to court organized
  • Plan make-ready only after legal possession
Local notes — Tahlequah / Wagoner / Tulsa Cherokee / Wagoner / Tulsa County procedures and wait times differ. Get local counsel — this section is orientation, not a DIY kit. STR removals mid-stay are usually platform + safety police issues, not FED court in the residential sense.

Turnovers & make-ready

Empty days are unpaid inventory. A repeatable make-ready sequence (clean → repair → paint → photo → list) beats heroic weekends.

Recommended tools

  • Placeholder: make-ready checklist template per unit type
  • Placeholder: photo standards for listing refresh

Forms

  • Vendor punch list with due dates
  • Final quality walk before marketing

Vendors

  • Cleaner, painter, carpet, handyman, photographer (or good phone photos)

Checklist

  • Same-day trash-out after keys
  • Repairs before paint; paint before final clean
  • Rekey; test HVAC, water heater, appliances
  • Update listing the day it’s truly ready — not before
Local notes — Tahlequah / Wagoner / Tulsa Student / seasonal timing around Tahlequah can cluster turnovers. Lake STRs: turnover is every checkout — SOP and cleaner reliability matter more than paint color. Keep a spare “guest-ready” kit (TP, bulbs, trash bags) for lake units.
